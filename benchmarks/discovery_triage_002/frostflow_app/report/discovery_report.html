Architecture Context

A full-stack Angular 19 SPA application for inventory and sales management (FrostFlow). Uses Supabase as BaaS for PostgreSQL database, authentication, and realtime subscriptions. Features role-based access (Admin, Staff) via auth guards. Multiple business modules: sales tracking, inventory management, reconciliation, AI-powered analytics, and staff management. Supports webhook integrations and includes dashboard metrics visualization with Chart.js.
